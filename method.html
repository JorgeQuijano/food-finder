01 · The equation

One formula. Every dish.

Calories set the denominator. Protein, fibre, sodium, sugar and saturated fat do the scoring. The result is rounded and capped at 0–100.

The full score all amounts are per serving 
nutrient_per_100 = amount ÷ calories × 100

raw_score =
    10                                           # base
  + 22 × min(protein_per_100 / 8, 1)
  + 22 × min(fibre_per_100 / 3, 1)
  + 20 × (1 − clamp((sodium_per_100 − 100) / 300, 0, 1))
  + 16 × (1 − clamp((sugar_per_100 − 1) / 4, 0, 1))
  + 10 × (1 − clamp((sat_fat_per_100 − 1.5) / 4.5, 0, 1))
  − fried_penalty
  − sugar_penalty
  − large_serving_penalty

score = round(clamp(raw_score, 0, 100))

A high protein-density or fibre-density dish earns more. A high sodium, sugar or saturated-fat density gives those points back.

Why this shape?

Density, not portion size.

A 250 kcal snack and a 700 kcal bowl should not be compared by grams alone. Dividing by energy lets them compete on the same terms.

  • Protein and fibre are the two biggest earners.
  • Sodium, sugar and saturated fat pull the score down as their density rises.
  • Penalties apply after the nutrient math, so fried or very sweet dishes cannot hide behind good macros.

The score is a comparison tool, not a grade on a person.

03 · The adjustments

Then a few points come off.

These adjustments stop a good macro split from covering for a fried, very sweet or oversized serving.

−8

Fried or crispy

Any dish tagged fried loses 8 points.

−0.6/g

Very sweet

Above 20 g of sugar, the score loses 0.6 points per extra gram, capped at −8.

−3

Large single serving

Dishes over 700 kcal lose 3 points. It is harder to fit them into a balanced day.

06 · The bands

What the number means.

80–100 · Excellent

A strong everyday pick with good protein/fibre density and moderate friction.

70–79 · Strong

Solid nutrition with one or two trade-offs worth noticing.

60–69 · Balanced

Fine in context. Pair it with something that fills the gaps.

Under 60 · Treat

More of a sometimes food in this comparison. Not a moral judgment.

Important caveats

What this score is not.

  • Not medical advice. Your needs depend on health, medications, activity and more.
  • Not an allergen check. Always confirm ingredients and cross-contact with the restaurant.
  • Not a complete diet score. Vitamins, minerals, omega-3s and food processing are not fully captured.
  • Not lab-tested. Menus and portions change; the nutrition figures are estimates.
  • Not a custom-order guarantee. Smart-swap suggestions estimate what could happen if the kitchen can adjust a sauce, cheese, protein or side. Confirm availability and allergens when you order.
