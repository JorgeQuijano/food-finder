How Forkful works

Forkful is a two-city demo guide for the GTA and London, built to make comparing food a little less mysterious. Every dish gets a 100-point score based on the nutrition estimate for one serving.

Why per 100 kcal?

It lets a small snack and a big bowl compete on the same terms. A dish earns the full protein score at 8 g of protein per 100 kcal and the full fibre score at 3 g per 100 kcal. Sodium is rewarded below 100 mg per 100 kcal, sugar below 1 g per 100 kcal, and saturated fat below 1.5 g per 100 kcal.

Fried dishes lose 8 points. Very sweet items get a small extra deduction, and single servings over 700 kcal lose 3 points. Scores are rounded and capped between 0 and 100.
